# Scout Lite: the no-install version

Use this when you just want the weekly brief inside Claude.

1. In Claude, open Settings, then Capabilities, and turn on web search.
2. Add `SKILL.md` from this folder as a skill (Settings › Capabilities › Skills › Upload), or create a Project and paste `SKILL.md` into its instructions.
3. Say: "Run my Scout for this week." The first time, it asks for your niche, your role, and the people and outlets you trust.

Every week after that, the same sentence gives you a new brief: the week on one page, what's in and what's out, the tools worth an hour, and four posts to make, each with its link and date.

Want the full Scout, with years of data history, X reading, charts, a narrated show and a 9:16 video? Install it from the top of this repo.
