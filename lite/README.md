# Scout Lite: the no-install version

Use this when you want the weekly brief inside Claude, with nothing to install on your computer.

1. In Claude, open **Settings › Capabilities** and turn on **Code execution and file creation** (called **Create and edit files** in newer settings). Skills need it.
2. Download **scout-lite.zip** from this folder (click it on GitHub, then the download button). Don't unzip it.
3. Go to **Customize › Skills**, click **+**, choose **+ Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**, and pick `scout-lite.zip`. Check that it's switched on.
4. In a new chat, make sure web search is on, then say: "Run my Scout for this week." The first time, it asks for your niche, your role, and the people and outlets you trust.

Every week after that, the same sentence gives you a new brief: the week on one page, what's in and what's out, the tools worth an hour, and four posts to make, each with its link and date.

Uploading skills works on Free, Pro and Max. On Team and Enterprise, your organization owner may need to allow uploaded skills. Prefer a Project? Create one and paste `SKILL.md` into its instructions instead.

Want the full Scout, with years of data history, X reading, charts and a designed PDF? Install it from the top of this repo.
